# Paper 4 human coding audit

- Sample: 198 segments; six coders; 197 complete cases.
- Fleiss' kappa (complete cases): 0.456 (bootstrap 95% CI 0.394–0.504).
- Krippendorff's alpha, nominal (all items, missing allowed): 0.458 (bootstrap 95% CI 0.408–0.522).
- Mean pairwise raw agreement: 55.2% (range 47.2%–83.3%).
- Mean pairwise Cohen's kappa: 0.463 (range 0.374–0.799).
- Strict-majority consensus: 123/198 segments (62.1%).
- Requires adjudication: 75/198 segments (37.9%).
- Participation-mode fields account for 66 of the 75 adjudication cases; all 66 participation items lack a strict majority under the substantive eight-code ontology.
- Senior adjudication resolves seven of the nine remaining substantive cases; two irreducibly multi-theme responses are excluded from single-label benchmarking.
- Final single-label gold standard: 130 substantive segments (123 strict-majority plus seven adjudicated).

The 130-item benchmark contains only strict-majority or explicitly adjudicated substantive labels. Participation-mode responses and the two irreducibly multi-theme substantive responses are excluded from the single-label benchmark.
